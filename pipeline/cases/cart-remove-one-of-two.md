# Removing one of two products leaves the other in the cart

Target: ui

## Steps

1. Log in as standard_user.
2. Add "Sauce Labs Backpack" to the cart.
3. Add "Sauce Labs Bike Light" to the cart.
4. Open the cart.
5. Remove "Sauce Labs Backpack" from the cart.

## Expected result

The cart lists only "Sauce Labs Bike Light", and the cart badge shows 1.
